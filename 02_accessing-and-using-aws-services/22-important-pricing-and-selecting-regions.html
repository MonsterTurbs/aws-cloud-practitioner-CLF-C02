22 — Important: Pricing & Selecting Regions 💸🌍

Reviewer / Study Guide (single-column, A4 print-ready)

🎯 Lecture Goal

Learn practical habits to avoid unexpected AWS costs and understand why Region selection matters for pricing and service compatibility.

Big idea ✅ To avoid surprise bills: regularly check your costs, use budgets with alerts, verify pricing pages, and pick the correct Region.

✅ Cost Safety Habits (Do These Throughout the Course)

The lecture strongly recommends that you continuously make sure you are not paying more than intended.

1) Monitor your monthly bill 🧾

  • Check the Billing Dashboard regularly
  • Review current month charges before the month ends

2) Use Budgets with alerts 🎯📧

  • Set a monthly budget (example: $10)
  • Get an email alert when you reach a threshold (example: 80%)
  • Reminder: budgets alert you—they don’t automatically stop spending

Beginner tip ✅ If you’re experimenting heavily, check costs daily until you’re confident.

🔎 Always Check Pricing Before Using a Service

Before starting a new AWS service, always verify how it is priced.

  • 📄 Visit the official service pricing page
  • 📏 Understand which units apply (per hour, per GB, per request, etc.)

Why this matters ✅ Different services (and even different features of the same service) can be priced differently.

🆓 Always Check the Free Tier Page

To reduce cost while learning, consult the AWS Free Tier page first:

  • Check if the service is included in Free Tier
  • Check what the quotas / limits are
  • Stay under those limits to avoid charges

Important ⚠️ “Free” usually means free within limits. Exceeding the quota can create costs.

🌍 Region Selection Affects Pricing and Compatibility

The lecture highlights that pricing can differ by Region for the same service.

💸 Pricing differences

The same AWS service may cost more or less depending on the Region.

🧭 Where you pick the Region

Usually in the top-right corner of the Management Console (Region selector).

Best practice ✅ Choose a Region intentionally (often based on cost, location, and requirements), and avoid accidental Region switching.

⚠️ Avoid Accidental Region Switching

The lecture warns that switching Regions accidentally can cause problems beyond just pricing:

  • 🔁 You might start using a more expensive Region without noticing
  • 🧩 Some services/resources might be in a different Region and therefore won’t work together as expected

Remember ⚠️ Many AWS services are regional. If two resources are in different Regions, they might not “see” each other or integrate easily.

🧠 Quick Checklist Before You Start Any New AWS Service

  1. 1) 🌍 Confirm the correct Region is selected (top-right Region selector)
  2. 2) 📄 Check the service pricing page
  3. 3) 🆓 Check the Free Tier page (quotas/limits)
  4. 4) 🎯 Ensure a Budget alert is configured (optional but recommended)
  5. 5) 🧾 Monitor Billing Dashboard during experimentation

🗂️ Key Terms (Exam Focus)

Billing Dashboard Budgets Budget Alerts Service Pricing Pages AWS Free Tier Quotas / Limits Region Region Selector Regional Services

✅ Quick Self-Check

Q1: What two tools are recommended to avoid unexpected costs?

A: Regularly check the monthly bill (Billing Dashboard) and use Budgets with alerts.

Q2: What should you do before using a service?

A: Check the service pricing page and the Free Tier page (limits/quotas).

Q3: Why does Region selection matter for pricing?

A: The same service can have different prices in different Regions.

Q4: Where do you typically select the Region in the console?

A: In the top-right Region dropdown (Region selector).

Q5: Why can accidental Region switching be a problem?

A: It can change costs and cause services/resources to not work together if they are in different Regions.
