20 — Get Started for Free with the AWS Free Tier 🆓☁️

Reviewer / Study Guide (single-column, A4 print-ready)

🎯 Lecture Goal

Understand what the AWS Free Tier is, how it helps new users learn AWS with low/no cost, and how the free usage is controlled via limits (quotas).

Big idea ✅ Many AWS services can be used for free within certain limits—especially during the first 12 months after creating your account.

🧾 Where to Find Free Tier Details

According to the lecture, you can find Free Tier information in two common ways:

  • 📌 On the AWS Pricing page (look for the Free Tier tab)
  • 🔎 Search for “AWS Free Tier” online

Beginner tip 💡 Always read the Free Tier page carefully—“free” usually means free up to a limit.

🆓 What “Free Tier” Means (Simple Explanation)

The Free Tier is AWS’s way of letting you explore many services at low/no cost. The lecture explains two big patterns:

1) 12 Months Free (for new accounts) ⏳

Many services are free to use within the first 12 months after account creation, as long as you stay below certain quotas.

2) Always Free (within limits) ♾️

Some services are free even after the first year, as long as you stay below certain limits.

Key concept ✅ Free Tier is not unlimited. It’s “free within quotas.”

📌 Examples Mentioned in the Lecture

Example A: Amazon EC2 (servers) 🖥️

EC2 can be used up to 750 hours per month for free (roughly a full month), as long as you use eligible instance types (e.g., t2.micro or t3.micro as shown in the lecture example).

Why this is helpful ✅ It lets you practice launching a virtual server without paying (if you stay within the limits and eligible types).

Example B: AWS Lambda (requests-based) ⚡

The lecture mentions that Lambda has an always-free style offer in the Free Tier: you won’t pay as long as you stay under limits such as 1 million requests per month (as stated in the lecture).

Why this is helpful ✅ You can experiment with running code in the cloud without managing servers, while staying in the free usage range.

Important ✅ The exact Free Tier limits vary per service and can change; always verify on the Free Tier page before relying on it.

⚠️ Free Tier “Rules” (How to Avoid Charges)

Free Tier safety checklist ⚠️

  • 📏 Stay below the Free Tier quota/limit for the service you’re using.
  • 🧩 Use eligible resource types (example: only specific EC2 instance types qualify).
  • ⏹️ Stop or delete resources you no longer need (so usage doesn’t accumulate).

Beginner mental model 💡 Free Tier is like a free monthly allowance. If you go beyond the allowance, you start paying.

🧠 Why the Free Tier Is Your Friend

The instructor emphasizes that Free Tier is especially useful when you’re learning AWS and following a course, because it helps you:

  • 🧪 Experiment with services and features
  • 📚 Learn by doing (hands-on practice)
  • 💸 Keep cost low as long as you respect limits

🗂️ Key Terms (Exam Focus)

AWS Free Tier Quotas / Limits 12 Months Free Always Free Eligible Resource Types EC2 (750 hours/month example) Lambda (1M requests/month example)

✅ Quick Self-Check

Q1: What is the AWS Free Tier?

A: A program that lets you use many AWS services for free within specific limits, especially during the first 12 months of a new account.

Q2: What are the two Free Tier patterns described?

A: Some services are free for the first 12 months (within quotas), and some are always free (within limits).

Q3: In the lecture example, how many EC2 hours per month can be free?

A: Up to 750 hours/month (with eligible instance types).

Q4: What is the main thing that controls whether you pay or not?

A: Staying below the Free Tier limits/quotas for the service.

Q5: Why is the Free Tier useful while learning?

A: It lets you experiment and practice hands-on at low/no cost as long as you respect the limits.
