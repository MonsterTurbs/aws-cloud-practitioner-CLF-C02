🏷️ AWS Official “Six Advantages of Cloud Computing”

The certification exam expects you to recognize these six advantages in AWS’s own wording. Official reference:

Official AWS page: Six advantages of cloud computing

1) Trade fixed expense for variable expense 💸

Meaning: Instead of paying fixed costs (data center + servers) upfront, you pay based on usage.

Connects to: Pay-as-you-go pricing.

2) Benefit from massive economies of scale 🏭

Meaning: AWS buys/operates hardware at huge scale, which can reduce per-unit cost and help lower prices.

Connects to: Cheaper than on-prem for many scenarios.

3) Stop guessing capacity 📊

Meaning: Avoid the “too much vs too little capacity” problem. Scale based on demand rather than forecasting hardware needs months ahead.

Connects to: Elasticity + scalability.

4) Increase speed and agility ⚡

Meaning: Provision resources quickly (minutes), so teams can experiment, build, and deliver faster.

Connects to: Agility (fast start).

5) Stop spending money running and maintaining data centers 🔧

Meaning: AWS runs the infrastructure so you can focus on business projects instead of “racking, stacking, powering servers.”

Connects to: Reduced maintenance burden; improved operational focus.

6) Go global in minutes 🌍

Meaning: Deploy in multiple regions quickly to reduce latency and improve resilience.

Connects to: Global reach + high availability.

🧩 Quick Mapping Cheat Sheet (Exam-Friendly)

  • Variable expense = pay-as-you-go usage billing
  • Economies of scale = AWS bulk buying/operations → lower unit costs
  • Stop guessing capacity = elasticity + scale on demand
  • Speed & agility = launch resources quickly
  • Stop maintaining data centers = AWS handles infra ops
  • Go global = deploy across regions for latency + availability

✅ Quick Self-Check

Q1: “Trade fixed expense for variable expense” refers to what concept?

A: Pay-as-you-go pricing (spend depends on usage instead of owning hardware).

Q2: What problem does “stop guessing capacity” solve?

A: Over-provisioning (idle waste) and under-provisioning (not enough capacity) by scaling on demand.

Q3: Which advantage matches “deploy around the world with a few clicks”?

A: Go global in minutes (global reach).

Q4: Why is “economies of scale” beneficial to customers?

A: AWS can achieve lower variable cost at large scale, which can translate to lower prices.
