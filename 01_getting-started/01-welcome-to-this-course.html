🧱 Beginner-Friendly Explanation: AWS & Cloud (Simple View)

AWS is a set of online services that let you use IT resources (servers, storage, databases, networking, etc.) without buying and maintaining physical hardware.

Example 💡

You want to host a website. Instead of buying a server machine, you “rent” computing power in AWS and pay only for what you use.

Why companies like it 📈

It’s faster to start, easier to scale, and reduces the burden of hardware maintenance.
