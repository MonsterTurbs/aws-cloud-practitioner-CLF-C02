English as a Second Language (ESL): Get +30 Minutes ⏱️

Reviewer / study guide for AWS exam-takers who are non-native English speakers. Single-column, A4 print-ready.

Core idea (memorize this) ✅

If you’re taking an AWS certification exam in a language that is not your native language, AWS offers an accommodation called “English as a Second Language (ESL)” that can add 30 extra minutes to your exam time.

Think of it as a time buffer for reading/processing questions in English (or another non-native language).

Official policy page 🔗

AWS Certification Policies (Before Testing) — English as a Second Language (ESL):
https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/before-testing/#English_as_a_second_language_

Notes: Policies can change. Always confirm the latest wording on the page above before booking or sitting the exam.

Who should request ESL time? 🗣️🙂

  • ✅ You are a non-native English speaker (or the exam language is not your native language).
  • ✅ You’ll take the exam in English (or another non-native language you choose).
  • ✅ You want extra time for reading long scenario questions, keywords, and tricky choices.

Important reminder ⚠️

ESL is an accommodation (time extension). It’s not a “hint” system and it doesn’t change the questions. It simply gives you more time to read, analyze, and answer.

How it helps (simple example) 🧠

Scenario: You get a long question about security + networking + cost. You understand the concepts, but it takes extra time to read.

Without ESL: You may rush and miss keywords like “most cost-effective”, “least operational overhead”, or “multi-account”.

With ESL (+30 min): You can re-read the question once, eliminate distractors, and still have time to review flagged items.

Practical checklist (use before booking) 🧾✅

  • Confirm you qualify as ESL (non-native language vs exam language).
  • Open the official policy page and read the ESL section.
  • When scheduling your exam, look for the ESL time accommodation option (wording depends on test provider).
  • Verify your appointment confirmation shows the extended time.
  • If your confirmation does not show extra time, contact the test provider support before exam day.

Pro tip 📌

Use the extra time strategically: flag tough questions, finish the easy ones first, then come back with a calm mind.

Mini self-quiz (CLF-C02 style) 🧩

  1. Question: Where do you confirm what you’re allowed to do and what accommodations exist for AWS exams?
    Expected answer: AWS certification policies (including the “Before Testing” policy page).
  2. Question: What does ESL accommodation change?
    Expected answer: It adds time (e.g., +30 minutes). It does not change questions or difficulty.

Summary (one-liner) ✅

If you’re a non-native English speaker taking AWS exams in English, you can typically request +30 minutes (ESL). Always confirm the latest rules on the official policy page.
